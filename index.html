Our Story

Rooted in
the Community

MaBhiza On Delivery was born out of a simple idea: the people of Ixopo deserve a delivery service they can actually depend on. Founded by Simlonde, the business grew from recognising a real gap in the community — that getting everyday things delivered quickly and affordably shouldn't be a luxury.

We operate right here in Ixopo, KwaZulu-Natal, with a deep understanding of the area, the roads, and the people. Every delivery is personal to us — because this is our home too.

Community-first approach — serving our neighbours with integrity
